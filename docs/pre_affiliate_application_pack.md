## 1. Product Positioning

Recommended public positioning:

> `[PROJECT_NAME]` is a visual fashion search and discovery site for Lithuanian shoppers. Users can search by style, category, color, price, and size, then click through to official retailer product pages.

Internal shorthand:

> Pinterest-like fashion discovery, but with buyable products from approved merchant feeds.

Do not use this as public wording:

> Shop like Pinterest.

Reason:

The product idea is useful internally, but a public slogan using another platform's brand can create unnecessary trademark and affiliation risk.

Better public phrases:

- Visual fashion discovery
- Shop by vibe
- From moodboard to wardrobe
- Find clothes by aesthetic
- Search fashion like you think about outfits
- Discover outfits, then shop the pieces

Reviewer-safe category:

```text
Content / shopping discovery / fashion search publisher.
```

Avoid choosing coupon, cashback, loyalty, browser extension, subnetwork, or media buying as the main publisher type unless that is actually how the site operates and the advertiser allows it.

Core promise:

```text
We help shoppers discover products. We do not replace the retailer, operate checkout, scrape retailer websites, or claim official partnership before approval.
```

## 3. Long Application Description

Use when a network or merchant asks for a fuller description.

```text
[PROJECT_NAME] helps shoppers discover fashion products across approved online stores in one visual search experience. The site focuses on high-intent fashion discovery pages, organic content, SEO, and curated search pages. Users can browse visual product cards, filter by attributes such as category, size, color, brand, price, and store, and then click through to the official retailer product page.

We do not operate checkout, resell products, or present ourselves as the merchant. Product data will be sourced from approved affiliate/product feeds or direct merchant permission. Affiliate links will be clearly disclosed, and we will follow each advertiser's program rules, including restrictions on paid search, brand bidding, coupon claims, social traffic, and deeplinking.
```

## 15. What Not To Say In Applications

Avoid:

- "We scrape fashion stores."
- "We will copy products from retailer websites."
- "We will run ads directly to affiliate links."
- "We will bid on Reserved/Sinsay/Sizeer/MODIVO keywords."
- "We are an official partner" before approval.
- "We are a coupon/discount site" unless applying as a coupon publisher and allowed.
- "We guarantee real-time prices" unless technically true.

Use instead:

- approved affiliate feeds
- official retailer product pages
- visual discovery
- high-intent fashion search
- SEO and organic content
- no checkout or resale
- clear affiliate disclosure

## 16. Follow-Up Templates

If a program asks for clarification:

```text
Thanks for reviewing [PROJECT_NAME]. To clarify, we are a fashion discovery/content publisher. We do not scrape retailer websites, operate checkout, or run direct affiliate arbitrage. Live merchant products will be shown only through approved product feeds, affiliate deeplinks, or direct permission. Traffic will be sent to our own discovery/search pages first, and outbound clicks will go to official retailer product pages through approved tracking links.
```

If approved and feed access is granted:

```text
Thank you for the approval. We will integrate the feed according to the program terms, keep affiliate disclosure visible, and ensure users click through to the official retailer product pages for checkout. Please let us know if there are any brand, product-feed, deeplink, or paid-traffic restrictions we should apply before launch.
```

If rejected:

```text
Thank you for the review. Could you share whether the rejection is due to site readiness, traffic sources, publisher type, geography, or another program requirement? We can adjust the application once the site is more mature or if specific compliance changes are needed.
```
