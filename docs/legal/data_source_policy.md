# Data Source Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-25

`Weft` is designed as an approved-feed fashion discovery site.

---

## 1. Product Data Sources

Products may be added from:

- approved affiliate product feeds
- approved affiliate deeplinks
- direct merchant permission
- merchant-provided CSV/XML/API exports
- manually provided merchant data
- mock or synthetic demo data clearly marked for demonstration

Products should not be added as active live catalog items without an approved source.

`Weft` is not intended to be a scraping-first catalog. We do not add live retailer catalog data by copying product pages without feed approval or direct permission.

---

## 2. What We Display

Depending on the approved source, product cards may display:

- product title
- brand
- store
- price
- old price or discount
- image
- category
- color
- size
- availability
- product link

---

## 3. Updates

Product data is updated periodically from the approved source. Prices and availability may change between updates. The retailer's website is the final source of truth.

Update frequency may depend on the feed, affiliate network, merchant rules, and technical limits. We do not guarantee real-time stock or price updates unless this is explicitly stated for a specific source.

---

## 4. Images And Descriptions

Product images and descriptions should be used according to the relevant feed terms, affiliate program terms, or direct merchant permission.

If permission is unclear, avoid storing full descriptions or caching images locally.

If a feed or program requires image hotlinking, local caching, attribution, removal, or specific display rules, those rules should be followed for that merchant.

---

## 5. Store Corrections Or Removal

Retailers can contact us for:

- corrections
- removal requests
- feed updates
- partnership questions
- attribution questions
- trademark or brand-use questions
- image or product-data concerns

Contact: `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`

When a retailer requests removal or correction, the relevant store or product records should be reviewed and paused or updated while the request is handled.

---

## 6. No Checkout

`Weft` does not operate checkout. Users click through to official retailer websites to purchase.

---

## 7. First-Wave Source Rule

For the first affiliate launch phase, live products should come only from the approved first-wave affiliate/feed sources:

- Reserved LT
- Sinsay LT
- Sizeer LT
- MODIVO LT
- Cropp LT
- ABOUT YOU LT

Other stores can be tracked as future candidates, but they should not appear as active live catalog sources until the correct program, feed, or direct permission is confirmed.

Factcool LT is monitoring-only for now because the official LT site currently states sales were suspended from 2025-03-06.
